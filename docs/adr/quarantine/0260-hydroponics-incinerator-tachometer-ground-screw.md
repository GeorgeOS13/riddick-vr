# ADR 0260: Subterranean Hydroponics Bio-Waste Incinerator High-Pressure Induced Draft Fan Rotor Dynamic Balance Optical Tachometer Cable Shield Grounding Lug Screw

## Status
Accepted

## Context
High static electrical charges generated by pyrolytic bio-waste dust storms inside the induced draft blower housing induce electromagnetic noise along the optical tachometer telemetry line. Torquing a braided grounding strap lug screw into the blower motor pedestal grounds the cable shield and guarantees noise-free balance phase telemetry.

## Decision
Implement physical tachometer cable shield grounding lug screw mechanics in `src/hook/camera_hook.cpp`:
- **Spatial Positioning**: Left hand braces the braided ground lug against the pedestal grounding boss (`leftLocal.z` between $-0.42\text{ m}$ and $-0.16\text{ m}$, `leftLocal.y` between $0.05\text{ m}$ and $0.35\text{ m}$, left grip $> 0.55$). Right hand aligns a hex driver at the grounding lug cap screw (`twoHandDist < 0.22\text{ m}`, right grip $> 0.55$, right trigger $< 0.40$).
- **Rotational Torquing Action**: Apply clockwise rotational wrist torque with the right controller (`|rVel.x| > 0.65\text{ m/s}`).
- **Synthesized Controls**: Emits `E` + `Q` + `Tab` + `Enter` to crush the star washer, torque the ground lug, and eliminate static interference.
- **Haptic Feedback**: $290\text{ Hz}$ ($0.98$) grounding screw torque stop on the right controller and $150\text{ Hz}$ ($0.60$) braided copper wire compression on the left controller.

## Consequences
- Eliminates electrostatic RF noise from incinerator blower telemetry.
- Seamlessly pairs with cable strain relief clamps, optical sensor alignment, and tape burnishing.
